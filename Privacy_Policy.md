# 📄 Privacy Policy – Cursus

*Last updated:* 2025

The *Cursus* app was developed with a commitment to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our app.

## 1. Collection and use of information

Cursus is designed to store most of your data locally on your device. We collect and use only the information strictly necessary for the following features to work:

- *Geolocation (geofencing):* For the automatic attendance-recording feature, the app uses *Apple's Geofencing service* to monitor when you enter and leave predefined areas (such as your university campus). Location data is processed *only on your device* and is *not stored* on our servers. Apple may collect anonymous, aggregated data in accordance with its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), which you may review.

- *Smart Schedule Reader:* To automatically add your courses from an image of your class schedule, Cursus uses the *Google Gemini API (free tier).* When you use this feature, the captured or selected image is securely sent to Google's servers for optical character recognition (OCR). *It is important to note that, under the [Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms?hl=pt-br) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=pt-br), because we use the free tier, Google may use submitted content (such as the schedule image) and the generated response to improve its products, services, and machine-learning technologies.* We recommend that you do not submit images containing excessively sensitive information through this feature. However, we highlight the security measures described in Google's own privacy terms.

- *Locally stored data:* All other information you create in the app —such as course names, grades, calculated averages, class photos, schedules, and configured locations—is stored *exclusively in your device's own storage.* We *do not have access* to that data.

## 2. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties, except to the essential service providers mentioned above (Apple and Google), and only for the purposes described. Those providers have their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

## 3. Security

We implement technical security measures to protect data while it is transmitted to third-party APIs. The security of the data stored on your device depends, in part, on your device's own settings and protections.

## 4. Your rights

Since the primary data remains on your device, you have full control over it. You may view, edit, or delete all of your information directly in the app interface. Deleting the app removes all local data. Your data will be backed up to your iCloud account, provided that storage is available, so it can be recovered when you reinstall the app or install it on a new device signed in to the same account.

## 5. Changes to this policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We recommend that you review this page from time to time to stay informed. The “Last updated” date at the top of this document will indicate when changes were made.
