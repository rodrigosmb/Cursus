# Cursus Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** October 20, 2026
**Effective:** from October 20, 2026

> This is a translation of the original Portuguese version. If the two differ, the Portuguese version prevails.

This Policy explains what personal data Cursus handles, for what purpose, with whom it is shared, how long it is kept and how you can exercise your rights. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018), the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (Law 12,965/2014) and the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Law 8,078/1990), and it is part of the [Terms of Use](Terms_of_use.md).

It applies to the Cursus app for iOS and, when available, to the Cursus website (together, the "**Platform**").

---

## Summary

- You sign in with your **Apple or Google** account. We do not ask for or store a password.
- We keep what you enter to organize your academic life: courses, grades, absences, classes, notebook, photos, PDFs and schedule. **This data stays in Brazil**, on Google Cloud servers in São Paulo.
- **We do not sell your data, show ads or track you** across other companies' apps and websites.
- **Google Analytics** collects usage data that does not identify you, to improve the app, and you can turn it off at any time in your Profile.
- The **Auto Readers** send the photo, PDF or text you choose to Google's artificial intelligence, in the United States, only when you use a Reader. Cursus does not keep this content.
- **Automatic Attendance** uses your location **only on the device**. Your position is not sent to us.
- If you connect the iPhone **Calendar** to the planner, events are read **only on the device**, to avoid scheduling conflicts. They are not sent to us.
- You can **delete your account and all your data from our servers** in the app itself, at any time.
- Cursus is for people **aged 18 or older**.

## 3. Data kept on your device

To work offline and faster, the app keeps on your iPhone:

- the sign-in session, in the iOS Keychain;
- a copy of your academic data, photos, PDFs and drawings;
- which Calendar calendars you chose to connect to the planner;
- preferences and Automatic Attendance information, such as course names, times, monitored locations and entry times.

This data stays only on the device and is erased when you delete the app. When you sign out of or delete the Account, the app stops showing its data, but some of these copies (such as photos and preferences) may remain on the device until you delete the app.

**If you used the old version of Cursus:** previously, the app kept everything only on the device and in your iCloud, and the previous policy said we had no access to this data. Now the data is kept in your Account, on our servers, as described in this Policy. For this reason, **data from the old version is only copied to your Account after you create the Account and accept these Terms and this Policy**. The copy is made only once, automatically, right after acceptance. The old data remains on the device until you delete the app. If you do not want to send the old data to the cloud, do not accept these Terms and this Policy: without acceptance, nothing is sent.

## 4. Google Analytics

We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to learn how the app is used, for example which screens are opened most and whether the Auto Readers work well, and so improve Cursus.

- **Based on legitimate interest, and you can turn it off.** Collection is on by default, because it serves only to improve the app itself, with data that does not identify you and without advertising (LGPD, art. 7, IX, and art. 10). If you do not want it, turn off *Profile → Privacy → Help improve Cursus*, with or without an account. Collection stops immediately, applies to the device and does not limit any feature.
- **What is sent:** usage events (such as "courses screen opened", "Apple sign-in chosen", "reading completed" or "reading failed") and technical data collected by the SDK itself: app installation identifier, device model, iOS and app version, language, approximate country or region, date of first launch, and session length and count.
- **What is not sent:** name, email, course names, grades, notes, photos, precise location or the content of readings. We also do not link Analytics data to your Account.
- **No advertising:** Google's advertising features are turned off and the app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
- **Where and for how long:** the data is processed by Google, including in the United States ([item 7](#7-who-we-share-data-with)). Event data is kept for **2 months** and then deleted automatically. When you turn off collection or delete the Account, the installation identifier is reset.
- To learn how Google handles this data: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en).

## 5. Device permissions

Cursus only asks for access to iPhone features when you use the function that needs them. You can deny or revoke any permission in **iOS Settings**, and the rest of the app keeps working.

- **Location**, for Automatic Attendance (optional):
  - When you turn on Automatic Attendance for a course, the app creates an area of about 50 meters around the class location.
  - During class time, iOS tells the app whether you entered or left this area, and the app records attendance or absence.
  - To work with the app closed, the "Always" permission is required.
  - **Your position is not sent to our servers and we do not keep a movement history.** Only the result (present or absent) goes into the course's attendance record.
  - You can turn off Automatic Attendance in the app or change the permission in Settings at any time.
  - Location and area monitoring are done by iOS Location Services, which Apple operates under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Camera**: to photograph the board or class materials and, if you want, the schedule or the syllabus for the Auto Readers.
- **Photos**: the app uses the iOS picker, so it only has access to the photos you choose, not to your whole library.
- **Files**: to attach PDFs to classes and the notebook and to send the syllabus to the Auto Readers. The app uses the iOS picker and only accesses the files you choose.
- **Calendar**, for the planner (optional):
  - When you connect the Calendar, iOS asks for access to your events, and you choose which calendars Cursus may take into account.
  - The app reads events from these calendars **only on the device**, so it does not schedule study sessions during busy times. The planner uses only the start and end of each event.
  - **Events are not sent to our servers.** Only the resulting study plan is saved in your Account.
  - You can disconnect the Calendar in the app or revoke the permission in Settings at any time.
- **Notifications**: to let you know about absences recorded automatically. These notifications are generated on the device itself.

## 6. Auto Readers (artificial intelligence)

The Auto Readers turn a photo of the class schedule, the syllabus (PDF or photos) or the text of the grading criteria into a draft that you review before saving.

- **Only when you ask.** Each Reader's screen states that the reading is done with Google's artificial intelligence. Nothing is sent unless you choose a file or text and request the reading; if you do not want to send anything, just do not use the Readers.
- **What is sent:** only the file or text you choose and a date reference (the year of the term or today's date), which helps interpret dates written without a year. We do not send your name, your email or any other data from your Account.
- **Who processes it:** the request goes to a service of ours hosted on **Google Cloud, in the United States**, which uses the **Google Gemini** artificial intelligence model through **Vertex AI**, Google Cloud's AI service. We use the paid, contracted version of the service, under which Google commits **not to use your content to train its models**. Google may keep technical logs for a limited time, only to prevent abuse and comply with the law.
- **What we keep:** the file sent is **not stored** by Cursus. It is processed and discarded. Only what you confirm in the draft is saved, such as courses, times and topics, plus the semester's reading count.
- **Human review and automated decisions:** the result is only a suggestion. It does not make decisions about you.
- **Do not send** documents with other people's data, identity documents or health information.

## 8. How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account, academic data, notebook, photos, PDFs, drawings, planner and acceptance of the Terms | While the Account exists. You can delete items at any time. |
| Files sent to the Auto Readers | Not stored by Cursus. They are discarded right after processing. |
| Google Analytics data | 2 months, then deleted automatically by Google |
| Server technical logs | Up to 30 days, except for security incident investigations or orders from an authority. Google's sign-in service keeps IP addresses for a few weeks. |
| Firebase installation identifier (used by remote configuration) | Until the Account or the app is deleted. After that, it leaves Google's backups within 180 days. |
| Support messages and privacy requests | Up to 5 years after the request is handled, to prove that we answered (period in art. 27 of the Consumer Protection Code) |
| Account deletion record | Indefinitely. It contains only the internal identifier, the status and the dates, without name or email |
| Data on the device | Until you delete the app |

**Account deletion:** in *Profile → Delete account*, you confirm the deletion and it starts immediately:
1. access is blocked;
2. we delete all your data in the database and all files (photos, PDFs and drawings);
3. if you sign in with Apple, we revoke the link with Sign in with Apple (the link with Google can be removed in your Google account settings);
4. we delete the Account.

The process usually finishes within minutes. If there is a technical failure, it is retried automatically during the first 24 hours and, if it still does not finish, we complete it manually within **30 days**. Cursus does not keep its own backups of your data. Google's systems erase technical backups of the sign-in service and of installation identifiers within **180 days**, under Firebase's policy. Only the deletion record described above and any data the law requires us to keep remain.

At the end of processing, we may keep data in **anonymized** form, that is, with no way to identify you, for statistics (LGPD, art. 16).

## 10. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can request at any time:

1. **confirmation** that we process your data;
2. **access** to your data;
3. **correction** of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
4. **anonymization, blocking or deletion** of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in breach of the LGPD;
5. **portability** of your data to another provider;
6. **deletion** of data processed based on your consent;
7. **information** about who we share your data with;
8. **information** about the possibility of not giving consent and the consequences of refusing;
9. **withdrawal of consent**;
10. **objection** to processing carried out on another legal basis, if the LGPD is not being complied with;
11. **review** of decisions made solely on the basis of automated processing. Cursus does not currently make decisions of this kind.

**How to exercise them:**
- **In the app itself:**
  - edit or delete any course, grade, class, note, photo, PDF or appointment;
  - disconnect the Calendar from the planner;
  - turn off Google Analytics in *Profile → Privacy*;
  - revoke permissions in iOS Settings;
  - delete the Account in *Profile → Delete account*.
- **By email**, at cursusapp06@gmail.com, for all other requests, including access to and a copy of your data. To protect you, we may ask that the request be sent from the same email as the Account or that you confirm your identity in another way.

Some requests may be refused: for example, if we cannot confirm your identity, if the request affects another person's rights or if the law requires us to keep the data. In these cases, we explain the reason in writing (art. 18, § 4).

**Response times:** we reply immediately in a simplified format, or within **15 days** with a complete statement (LGPD, art. 19). The service is free of charge.

If you believe we did not handle your request properly, you can file a complaint with Brazil's **National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)** at [gov.br/anpd](https://www.gov.br/anpd) or with consumer protection bodies.

## 12. Security

We take technical and administrative measures to protect your data:

- all communication between the app and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and data is encrypted at rest on Google's servers;
- sign-in only through Apple or Google, with no password stored by us;
- access rules that allow **only you**, once authenticated, to read and change your data. No data is public and there is no sharing between users;
- administrative access restricted to the team members who need it.

No system is completely immune to failures. If a security incident occurs that may pose a relevant risk or harm to you, we will notify the ANPD and the affected people within **3 business days**, as required by ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024.
